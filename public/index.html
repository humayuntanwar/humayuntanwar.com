Hyderabad, Pakistan

I'm from Hyderabad, Pakistan.

Where I grew up, hardly anyone makes it to university.

The train north

I made it, on a scholarship, to study software engineering in Islamabad.

Home became a 24-hour train ride away.

Islamabad

Four years of hostel life, far from my family.

Then I graduated.

Karachi

Yet another move, this time to Karachi.

I joined a fintech as an Android developer and rebuilt its app from the inside.

Somewhere in there, I fell in love with payments.

Dubai, 2020

Then a life-changing chance: Dubai. I came alone, again.

Then Covid hit.

Ten-hour days in isolation, for a year.

Dubai

Things eased. The hours stayed hard.

But I started exploring the city, and I loved it.

In two and a half years I learned more than I could have in ten.

Further

I moved to a startup. It made me clearer, and more confident.

I started travelling. Istanbul, Baku, Athens, Rome.

Asia

Two years in, the company was acquired.

I became obsessed with helping more businesses understand and accept payments, in more markets.

Without any official title, I took on Asia.

Now

Four and a half years in, the recognition came.

Officially: I lead integrations for EMEA.

It all started at one dot on this map.

I'm Humayun. Say hello, or find me on LinkedIn.
